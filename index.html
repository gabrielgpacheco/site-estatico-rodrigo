about

Rodrigo Sant’Ana is a producer, director and certified broadcaster, with experience in different entertainment environments, such as musical theater, music shows, film productions and broadcasting.

He studied Arts at Universidade Federal Fluminense, where he enhanced his artistic sensibility and aligned practical and theoretical knowledge in visual and performing arts, applying dramaturgy studies and aesthetic concepts of cinema as powerful differentials in his work as a storyteller.

Read more

He was assistant director in miniseries such as Santos Dumont – O Desafio do Ar, directed by Adolpho Rosenthal and in advertising films such as Unilever – Sun by the award-winning duo of directors, 300ml.

He signed the cast production of Festa Burn campaign, an advertising piece for Rock in Rio, directed by 300ml. He produced the casting of extras for TV series Mandrake – HBO Latin America by Conspiração Filmes and was casting assistant for the Bollywood production, Dhoom 2 – Yash Raj Films in partnership with Utopia Films Brazil.

He was a managing partner of the talent agency Trip Casting and provided services for film production companies and TV broadcasters such as Rede Globo, TV Record, Conspiração Filmes, Hungryman, Cine C, Mixer, Utopia Films, and others.

As Filmmaker, he works in the shooting and editing for short films, music videos, wedding films, corporate and institutional videos, having worked with companies such as Ateliê Multimídia, Re9 Filmes, Allados Studio, Pet Media, and others.

As stage manager and AV technician, he is responsible for the technical operation of the shows including Moana, Wizard of OZ, Peter Pan and Alice in Wonderland with Cia Teatral Sifuxipá.

In addition to being a backstage professional, he is also a performance artist and percussionist musician.
